01 / Challenge

Turning strong awareness into confident customer commitment

EV content across the industry was becoming louder, denser and more technical at the very moment customers were forming high-stakes decisions. The task was to convert strong awareness into confident commitment.

02 / What success required

What success required

01

Confidence

Strengthen customer confidence at key decision moments so sign-up and reservation felt like natural progressions, not pressured commitments.

02

Insight

Use behavioural data and cross-market analysis to understand why progression stalled despite strong awareness and traffic.

03

Ensure optimisation improvements performed reliably across multiple European markets, not isolated gains in single regions.

05

03 / Experience strategy

Designing optimisation around customer readiness

01

Analysis

Performance analysis across European markets revealed consistent drop-off at key commitment stages, even where earlier engagement was strong.

02

Hypothesis

Each optimisation was tied to a central behavioural question: what decision is the customer making here, and what reassurance do they need to move forward?

03

Testing

Controlled A/B testing focused on moments of commitment — value positioning before sign-up, improved sequencing, and reduced friction.

04 / Optimisation

Learning through disciplined experimentation

01

Sequencing

Content sequencing was refined to customer readiness — reassurance and value introduced earlier so commitment requests arrived at the right moment.

02

Clarity

Messaging hierarchy was simplified to reduce cognitive friction, so the value of signing up or reserving was clear without extra layers.

03

Completion

Form interactions were redesigned to remove barriers to completion while keeping brand restraint, reducing hesitation at the final step.
